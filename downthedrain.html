Find your flow.

There’s no rush and no perfect way to play. Follow the water, try something new, and enjoy the last little scrap disappearing.

  • Water that moves.The spray pools, swirls, and carries scraps toward the drain. Every rinse has its own rhythm.
  • Your spray, your way.Fan or Jet. Gentle or strong. Hot or cold. Add some soap, plug the drain, then pull the stopper.
  • A change of scenery.Ten sinks, from a kitchen after pasta night to a paint studio, an espresso bar, and a garden tub.
  • Stay a little longer.Play Classic, tackle dishes in Challenge, or mix it up in Random. Endless keeps the mess coming.
  • Made to feel good.Satisfying sounds, gentle vibration, and Apple Pencil support make every spray feel a little more hands-on.

Good to know

A few small details.

What can I play for free?

The Kitchen, Bar sink, and Granite are free in Classic mode. A single purchase unlocks the seven other sinks and Challenge, Random, and Endless modes permanently.

Are there time limits or ads?

No. There are no time limits, scores, or ads. Take as long as you like, or switch on Endless to keep rinsing.

Which devices can I use?

Down the Drain is made for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. You can play by touch, and Apple Pencil is supported on compatible iPads.

How do I get help?

Visit our support page or email support@zaftheory.com. Tell us your device, operating system version, and what happened so we can help.
