About the game

Turn a messy sink into a small moment of calm. Drag to spray, watch the water flow, and guide every crumb, pea and noodle down the drain.

  • Flowing water: the spray pools, swirls and carries scraps toward the drain.
  • Ten sinks, ten kinds of mess: from a kitchen after pasta night to a paint studio, an espresso bar and a garden tub.
  • Your water, your way: Fan or Jet, gentle or strong pressure, hot or cold. Pump some soap, plug the drain to fill the sink, then pull the plug.
  • Three ways to play: Classic, Challenge with dishes in the way, and Random. Switch on Endless and the mess keeps coming for as long as you like.
  • Made to feel good: satisfying sounds and gentle vibration made for the game, plus Game Center achievements and Apple Pencil support.
  • No pressure: no time limits, no score, no ads. Just spray.
