Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

At ZAF Theory LLC (“we”, “our”, or “us”), we believe great software doesn't need to track you. Our applications are built with a privacy-first approach: we don't collect, sell, or rent your personal information.

1. Data Collection & Usage

We design our applications and games to operate without collecting personal information:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers through our software.
  • Local Storage Only: All game saves, progression states, settings, and configurations are stored on your local device (such as within the iOS, iPadOS, or macOS application sandbox) and are not sent to us. The one exception is Game Center achievement progress in games that offer it, described in section 2.
  • Update Checks: Some of our Mac apps can check for a newer version by loading their page on zaftheory.com, for example when you open their About screen. That is an ordinary page request, covered by Website Privacy below, and the app sends nothing else with it.
  • No Analytics or Ads: We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics frameworks in our applications.
  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not share data from our apps and games with third parties, except for achievement progress sent to Apple when you use optional Game Center features, as described in section 2. We do not share data for advertising or marketing.

2. App Store Purchases & Apple Services

If you purchase or download our applications via the Apple App Store:

  • All payments, billing, and downloads are handled directly by Apple Inc. in accordance with the Apple Privacy Policy. ZAF Theory LLC never receives or stores your credit card or financial details.
  • If you have opted in to share diagnostic and usage data with app developers via your Apple device settings (iOS, iPadOS, or macOS), Apple may provide us with aggregate, anonymized crash logs. These logs do not contain personally identifying information and are used solely to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Game Center: Some of our games offer Game Center achievements. Game Center is optional and run by Apple. If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievement progress is reported to Apple and appears in your Game Center profile. Apple processes this data under its own policy, described in Game Center & Privacy. ZAF Theory LLC does not receive your name, email address, or other personal information from Game Center. To keep each player's progress separate on a shared device, games that offer Game Center also store achievement progress on your device under Game Center's player identifier; this stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can stop sharing achievement progress by signing out of Game Center in the Settings app.

3. Support & Email Communications

If you voluntarily email us for technical support or feedback (e.g. at support@zaftheory.com):

  • We use your email address and message contents solely to address your questions and assist you.
  • We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties, nor will we add you to marketing mailing lists.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

In Down the Drain, game progress, settings, and locally stored achievement progress stay on your device until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data; we do not hold a copy.

Achievement progress sent to Game Center is kept by Apple under its own policy. You can stop sharing achievement progress by signing out of Game Center in the Settings app. Signing out or deleting the game does not delete achievement progress already held by Apple.

We keep support emails only as long as we need them to help you. To request deletion of your support emails, email support@zaftheory.com.

5. Website Privacy

Our website (zaftheory.com) is a static site hosted via GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, user tracking scripts, or analytics services. GitHub may collect technical connection data (such as visitor IP addresses) for security and operational purposes as detailed in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children's Privacy

Because our applications and services do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our apps or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our software, please get in touch.
