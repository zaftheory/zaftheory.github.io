Help & Contact

Apps & game support

Have a question, feedback, or need help with one of our apps or games? We're here to help.

Direct Support Contact

For bug reports, troubleshooting, or feature requests, please reach out directly:

Email support@zaftheory.com

We are an independent studio and aim to respond to all inquiries within 1–2 business days.

How to Get Help

When contacting support regarding an issue or bug, including the following information helps us resolve it faster:

  • App & Platform: The name of the application and your operating system version.
  • Description: A brief description of what happened, what you expected to happen, and steps to reproduce.
  • Screenshots / Logs: Any screenshots, screen recordings, or error messages if applicable.

General & Business Inquiries

For press, business inquiries, or other questions, you can reach us at support@zaftheory.com.
